How to create a QR code for a Zoho Sign document

The fastest way to collect a signature from someone standing in front of you isn't email — it's a QR code they scan with the phone already in their hand. Here's how to turn any Zoho Sign template into a print-ready QR code that works on posters, clipboards, counters, and job-site signage.

Where signing QR codes earn their keep

  • Waivers & releases — gyms, rentals, events, activity businesses. Scan at the front desk, sign before you start.
  • Work authorizations — field service and contracting. The customer signs the estimate approval at the kitchen table, on their own phone.
  • Intake forms — clinics, law offices, salons. The clipboard becomes a placard with a QR code.
  • HR paperwork — onboarding packets and policy acknowledgments posted in the break room.
  • Open houses & events — sign-in sheets and NDAs without a laptop station.

The setup (about 5 minutes)

Step 1 — Connect Zoho Sign

Create a SignFlow Pro account and connect your Zoho Sign API credentials (any plan with API access works — no Enterprise upgrade required).

Step 2 — Publish a template as a public form

Pick the template, choose which signer role the public fills, and optionally brand the landing page with your logo and colors.

Step 3 — Download the QR code

Every form comes with a print-ready QR code. Drop it into a flyer, table tent, yard sign, or laminated counter card.

Step 4 — People scan and sign

The code opens your branded signing page. Signers enter their name and email and complete the document through Zoho Sign's standard flow — full audit trail, legally binding, and no Zoho account required.

Reprint-proof: SignFlow Pro QR codes point at a stable redirect ID, not the form's display URL. Rename the form or change its slug later — the printed code keeps working. You'll never have to chase down and replace old signage.

Know if the signage is working

Each form tracks visits, submissions, and conversion rate. If the poster at location A gets 40 scans and 35 signatures while location B gets 3 scans, you know where to fix the placement — this is the difference between a QR code and a QR code program.
