How to create a shareable link for a Zoho Sign template

You have a document people sign over and over — a client agreement, a liability waiver, an intake form. Sending a fresh Zoho Sign request to every single person is slow. What you want is one permanent link you can put in an email signature, on your website, or behind a QR code. Here's how to get one.

The problem: Zoho Sign requests are one-to-one

Out of the box, Zoho Sign works like a mail merge: you pick a template, type in the recipient's name and email, and send. That's fine for contracts you negotiate individually. It breaks down when the signer should initiate — walk-in customers, new hires, event attendees, patients, rental applicants.

Zoho's native answer is SignForms, but it's gated to the top-tier plan. If you're on a lower plan, the choices are: upgrade every user to Enterprise, or use the Zoho Sign API.

Creating a shareable link with SignFlow Pro

Step 1 — Enable API access in Zoho Sign

Your Zoho Sign plan needs API access. In the Zoho API console, create a client (a client ID and secret). This is a one-time setup.

Step 2 — Connect your account

Create a SignFlow Pro account at signflow.ink and paste in your Zoho credentials. They're encrypted at rest, and all signing still happens through Zoho's own infrastructure — SignFlow Pro never touches your signed documents.

Step 3 — Pick a template and a signer role

SignFlow Pro lists the templates already in your Zoho Sign account. Choose one, pick which signer role the public link should fill (multi-role templates are supported — you can route the other roles by email automatically), and customize the landing page with your logo and colors if you like.

Step 4 — Publish and share

You get a permanent URL like signflow.ink/your-form-name — plus a matching print-ready QR code. Anyone who opens the link enters their name and email and signs. The link never expires and never needs re-sending.

Every link comes with built-in analytics: visits, submissions, and conversion rate, so you know whether the people you're pointing at the form actually finish it.

What signers see

A clean, branded page with your logo, the form name, and two fields: name and email. They click through into Zoho Sign's standard signing flow. No Zoho account, no signup, no app download. When they finish, the completed document lands in your Zoho Sign account like any other envelope.
