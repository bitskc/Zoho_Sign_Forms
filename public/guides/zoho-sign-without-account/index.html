How to let anyone sign a Zoho Sign document — no Zoho account required

Good news first: your signers never needed a Zoho account. The real bottleneck is on your side — someone on your team has to create and send every single request. Here's how to remove that step entirely.

How Zoho Sign handles external signers today

When you send a document through Zoho Sign, the recipient gets an email with a signing link. They click it, sign in the browser, done — no account, no download. That part works well.

The friction is upstream. For every signature you want to collect, a licensed Zoho Sign user has to open the app, pick the template, type the recipient's name and email, and hit send. Multiply that by every walk-in customer, every new hire, every event attendee, and you've turned your e-signature tool into a data-entry job.

Flip the direction: let signers start

The fix is a public signing link — one permanent URL where the signer enters their own name and email and the document is generated and presented automatically. Nobody on your team touches it.

Zoho's native version of this (SignForms) is reserved for its top-tier plan. SignFlow Pro provides the same capability on any Zoho Sign plan with API access, for $60/year flat:

  1. Connect your Zoho Sign API credentials (encrypted at rest).
  2. Pick a template and which signer role the public fills.
  3. Share the permanent link — or its print-ready QR code.

The signer opens the page, types their name and email, and signs through Zoho Sign's standard flow. Completed documents land in your Zoho Sign account with the usual certificate and audit trail.

Multi-role templates work too. If your agreement needs a countersignature from your side, SignFlow Pro routes the remaining roles by email automatically after the public signer finishes.

Common scenarios this unblocks

  • Client onboarding agreements linked from your proposal or email signature
  • Liability waivers signed on the customer's own phone at the counter
  • Vendor W-9s and NDAs — send one link instead of managing requests
  • Volunteer and event forms shared in a group chat or newsletter
