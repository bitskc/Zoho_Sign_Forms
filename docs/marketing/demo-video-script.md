## Recording notes
- Use a demo Zoho account with realistic template names ("Client Services Agreement", "Liability Waiver") — not "Test Template 3".
- Scene 5 on a real phone screen-recording sells the "no account" claim better than a resized browser window.
- Keep cursor movement slow; viewers follow the cursor.
- 1080p minimum; hide bookmarks bar.
